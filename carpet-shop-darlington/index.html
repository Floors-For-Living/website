Questions

Do you fit carpets in Darlington?

Yes. Our network of local fitters covers Darlington and the villages around it, including Cockerton, Hurworth, Heighington and Middleton St George. If you’d rather fit it yourself, we can supply only.

Is it worth driving to Barnard Castle for a carpet?

We think so, but we would. You get a proper independent showroom, premium British brands, and people who’ll help you choose rather than sell you whatever’s on offer. Plus Barnard Castle is a lovely place for a coffee afterwards.

Can I take samples home?

Yes. Carpet always looks different at home than it does in a showroom, so we’d rather you see it in your own rooms first.

What flooring do you sell?

Carpet, LVT, vinyl, real wood and bespoke pieces such as stair runners, from premium brands.
