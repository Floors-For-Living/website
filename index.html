Welcome

Proper floors, fitted properly

Floors for Living is a new flooring showroom in Barnard Castle. Come in, walk on the floors, and talk to people who fit them for a living.

We stock LVT, carpet and vinyl from brands we trust, and when a standard floor won't work we'll make something that does. Every job is measured and fitted by our own team, so the people who quote are the people who turn up.
