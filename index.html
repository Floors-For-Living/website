The finishing touches

Available whether you’re having your floor fitted or buying supply only, so everything comes from one place.

  • Underlay

    Softer and warmer underfoot, and it helps your floor wear well for years longer.

  • Door bars and thresholds

    A neat, safe join where two floors meet, so edges don’t lift, fray or trip anyone.

  • Stair nosings

    Protects the edge of every step, where stairs wear first, and gives a clean finish.

  • Gripper and fitting accessories

    The unglamorous bits that keep carpet tight, flat and free of wrinkles.

  • Adhesives and smoothing compound

    A flat, properly bonded base is what makes a new floor look right and stay put.
