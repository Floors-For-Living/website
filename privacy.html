We are the data controller. That means we decide how your details are used and we are responsible for looking after them.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry through the form on our website, we collect:

  • your first name and phone number
  • your email address, if you choose to give it
  • your message
  • your postcode and the types of floor you are interested in, if you tell us
  • how you heard about us, if you choose to tell us

The form also sends us some details automatically:

  • the advert or campaign that brought you here (campaign tags in the link you clicked)
  • advertising click IDs from Facebook or Google
  • the website you came from
  • the page you landed on

We also use your IP address to check that you are a real person and to limit spam. We do not store the address itself. We store only a one-way scrambled (hashed) version of it.

Why we use your details

  • To reply to your enquiry and take steps you ask for before any contract. Our legal basis is legitimate interests and pre-contract steps.
  • To understand which of our advertising and channels bring us enquiries. Our legal basis is legitimate interests.
  • To keep the site secure and free of spam. Our legal basis is legitimate interests.

We do not sell your data. We do not send you marketing from this form.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are deleted automatically after 12 months.

If you go on to buy from us, we keep the records we need for your order, guarantees and our accounts separately. We keep those for as long as the law and the job require.

Who handles your data for us

These companies process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase runs our database, which is hosted in Ireland.
  • Resend sends our enquiry email notifications.
  • Cloudflare runs the Turnstile spam check on the form.
  • GitHub hosts the website.
  • GoDaddy looks after our domain and email.

Some of these are US companies, so your data may be transferred outside the UK. When that happens, it is protected by the safeguards the UK GDPR requires.

Our website also loads Google Maps and Google Fonts. That means Google can receive your IP address when those pages load.

Cookies

We do not set any cookies ourselves. The enquiry form does not use cookies or browser storage. The campaign details described above are held in the page only while you fill in the form.

The Google map and the Cloudflare spam check are run by those companies. They may use their own cookies or similar technologies when they load. We do not control those.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the details we hold about you
  • ask us to correct anything that is wrong
  • ask us to delete your details
  • ask us to restrict how we use them
  • object to how we use them

To use any of these rights, email sales@floorsforliving.co.uk or phone 01833 695875. We aim to reply within one month.

If you are not happy with how we have handled your details, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
